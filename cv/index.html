Professional Summary

Experienced in system modeling and big data analytics with a strong Physics and Mathematics foundation. Since 2021, proven expertise in clinical application systems implementation and optimization. Passionate interdisciplinary problem solver with expertise in image post-processing, data analysis, and statistical modeling across multiple programming languages. Proven ability to integrate diverse techniques and collaborate effectively in teams while adapting to new challenges and technologies. Topics: big data processing and analysis, deep learning, advanced modeling, breast cancer radiotherapy, probabilistic segmentation, fluid dynamics, multi-wavelength imaging.
