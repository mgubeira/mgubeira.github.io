**Full title:** Predicting toxicities after Whole-pelvis RT for prostate cancer  
**Funding:** AIRC IG 14603 (new AIRC IG in preparation)  
**Period:** 2014-present  
**PI:** C. Cozzarini

### Description

A long-standing prospective multicentric study involving more than 15 Italian institutes and over 850 enrolled patients, led by Dr. Cozzarini (Radiation Oncology Department). It is one of the largest projects worldwide collecting real-life, patient-reported information on toxicities after radiotherapy for prostate cancer, with the aim of developing predictive models to guide future prostate cancer radiotherapy.
