**Funding:** Ricerca Finalizzata  
**Period:** 2023-2026  
**PI:** C. Doglioni; **co-PI:** C. Fiorino

### Description

The project addresses the transition from traditional diagnosis on glass slides to digital slides in pathology. The first goal is to compare the two approaches, focusing on cases of disagreement. Several commercial systems are also evaluated for their quality control and automated diagnosis capabilities, and a federated learning pipeline is being developed to detect low-quality digitized slides.

Follow-up activities include deep learning-based image classification and segmentation on digital slides, and new methods for nuclei counting via statistical thresholding.
