**Full title:** MAcklin effect, quantitative imaging analysis and CytoKine profiling to predict Lung frailty IN ARDS  
**Funding:** NextGenerationEU, PNRR 12376796, and Ricerca Finalizzata  
**Period:** 2023-2026  
**PI:** D. Palumbo, G. Landoni; **co-PI:** M. Mori

### Description

The high incidence of barotrauma in COVID-19-related acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) suggests that these patients exemplify lung frailty. Previous research from our institutions identified the Macklin effect as a key radiological predictor of barotrauma in COVID-19 ARDS, and densitometry-based methods were developed to identify high-risk patients. By combining these methods with advanced AI, the project aims to identify imaging biomarkers for non-invasive lung frailty assessment in both COVID-19 and non-COVID-19 ARDS patients, and to validate them in an external cohort, providing a tool to identify ARDS patients at high risk of barotrauma.
