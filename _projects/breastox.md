**Funding:** AIRC grant  
**Period:** 2026-2030  
**PI:** C. Fiorino

### Background

Skin/breast (S/B) irradiation causes late morbidities after adjuvant radiotherapy (RT) for breast cancer, and cardiovascular diseases (CVD) are also relevant long-term complications. A large single-institution cohort followed prospectively (more than 2500 patients) allows the development of robust risk prediction models. Validation on independent cohorts will assess their generalizability and usability for personalized care, while the inclusion of imaging biomarkers can further improve risk assessment.

### Aims

1. Developing and externally validating predictive models for S/B and CVD toxicities.
2. Quantifying the additional value of CT imaging-based quantitative scores (densitometric/radiomic).
3. Exploring actions to mitigate S/B/CVD toxicities, focusing on high-risk patients.
