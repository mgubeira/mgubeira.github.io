**Full title:** Risk assessment tools for severe side effects after breast radiotherapy: radiation safety through biological extended models and digital twins  
**Funding:** EU project (Euratom)  
**Period:** 2024-2027  
**PI:** T. Rancati; **WP PI:** C. Fiorino

### Description

In this multi-institutional project, our work package extracts quantitative CT image-based biomarkers to be included in the risk scores developed by another project partner and integrated into a common platform. The main focus is on cardiac and pulmonary late side effects.
