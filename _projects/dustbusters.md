### Description

Planet formation occurs in thin, dense protostellar discs of gas and dust orbiting young stars. High-resolution facilities such as ALMA (sub-mm) and SPHERE at the VLT (near-IR) now allow these discs to be observed at a spatial resolution of a few astronomical units. The project aims to strengthen collaboration among groups in Europe, the USA, Chile and Australia in order to (1) develop numerical algorithms to study how newborn planets interact with their gas and dust environment, and (2) compare these models with state-of-the-art IR and sub-mm observations of protostellar discs.
