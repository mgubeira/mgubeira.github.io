**Full title:** AI-based CTV Segmentation in Breast Radiotherapy: Exploration of Large-Scale Applicable Models, Including Intra- and Inter-Center Probability Maps  
**Funding:** Ministry of Health, CCM 2024  
**Period:** 2025-2027  
**Partners:** Istituto Superiore di Sanità (ISS); MP-DReAM Group, IRCCS Ospedale San Raffaele (Milan); Regina Elena Cancer Institute (Rome); Federico II University Hospital (Naples); Fondazione Policlinico Universitario Agostino Gemelli IRCCS (Rome)

### Aims

1. a. Generation of breast CTV contouring models including inter-observer delineation uncertainties, in the form of probability maps.  
   b. Development of a software platform to facilitate access to and use of the models in future clinical and research applications (e.g., clinical trial QA, tutoring and educational tools).
2. Model validation across 4 centers, evaluating the agreement with the automatically generated probability maps.
3. Additional validation on 4 further selected radiotherapy centers and final development of the inter-center probabilistic model, demonstrating the generalizability of the proposed models with respect to inter-institutional variability.
