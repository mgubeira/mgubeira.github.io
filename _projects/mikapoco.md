**Full title:** Improving the quality of Radiotherapy by multi-Institution Knowledge-Based planning optimization models  
**Funding:** AIRC grant IG 23150  
**Period:** 2020-2025  
**PI:** C. Fiorino

### Description

Multi-Institutional Knowledge-based Approach in Planning Optimization for the Community (MIKAPOCo) involves numerous national and international research groups. The project develops strategies to improve radiotherapy treatments of oncology patients through AI-based optimization, using knowledge-based approaches built on multi-institutional experience. Major goals include large-scale plan optimization, plan audit, plan QA and support to clinical trials, tutoring and educational projects.
