### Description

This thesis validates a probabilistic breast CTV map, derived from an ensemble of transfer learning models, against manual delineations performed by multiple centres on the same patients, each used as a centre-specific reference. Agreement is assessed per iso-probability level through cumulative metrics, Voronoi-zone ECDF, DICE, HD95 and ASD, while each patient's map is characterised by standard deviation, skewness and kurtosis.
